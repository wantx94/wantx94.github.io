《咕咕養雞場》隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日

《咕咕養雞場》（以下稱「本遊戲」）由個人開發者 wantx94（以下稱「我們」）開發。本政策說明本遊戲如何處理你的資料。

1. 本遊戲不蒐集你的個人資料

  • 不需要註冊或登入帳號。
  • 遊戲進度（雞、蛋、金幣等）只存在你的裝置上，不會傳到我們這裡。我們沒有架設伺服器，也拿不到你的遊戲資料。
  • 本遊戲不使用分析工具，也不讀取你的聯絡人、照片、相機、麥克風或精確位置。

2. 廣告（Google AdMob）

本遊戲有「看廣告換獎勵」的功能，廣告由 Google 的 Google AdMob 提供。播放廣告時，AdMob 可能會蒐集並處理下列資料：

  • 裝置的廣告識別碼（iPhone 的 IDFA、Android 的廣告 ID）及其他裝置識別碼
  • IP 位址，以及由 IP 位址推估的大略位置
  • 裝置與系統資訊（例如機型、作業系統版本、語言）
  • 與廣告的互動紀錄（例如看了哪些廣告、是否點擊）
  • 當機與效能等診斷資料

這些資料由 Google 依其政策用於投放與衡量廣告、防止詐騙與濫用。詳細說明請見：

  • Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資料：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google 隱私權政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. 同意與追蹤

  • 在歐洲經濟區、英國、瑞士等依法需要取得同意的地區，第一次開啟本遊戲時會先顯示 Google 的同意視窗，你可以選擇是否同意。
  • 在 iPhone 上，本遊戲會詢問是否允許追蹤。不允許也能照常遊玩，只是看到的廣告比較不相關（非個人化廣告）。
  • 看廣告是自願的，不看廣告也能玩。

4. 你的選擇

  • 更改廣告同意（需要同意的地區）：在遊戲中點右上角的「？」，於說明頁點「廣告隱私設定」。
  • 關閉 iPhone 的追蹤：設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤，關閉《咕咕養雞場》。
  • 重設或刪除 Android 的廣告 ID：設定 → Google → 廣告。
  • 調整 Google 的廣告個人化：https://adssettings.google.com

5. 兒童

本遊戲不是為 13 歲以下的兒童設計，我們不會故意蒐集兒童的個人資料。如果你認為有兒童的資料被蒐集，請與我們聯絡。

6. 資料的保存與刪除

  • 遊戲進度存在你的裝置上，刪除本遊戲就會一併刪除；也可以在遊戲說明頁按「重新開始」清空進度。
  • AdMob 蒐集的資料由 Google 依其政策保存與處理，你可以透過上面 Google 的連結管理。

7. 政策變更

本政策如有修改，我們會更新本頁內容與上方的生效日期。

8. 聯絡我們

有任何問題，請寫信到 wantx94@gmail.com。

Chicken Farm Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Chicken Farm (咕咕養雞場, "the game") is developed by wantx94, an individual developer ("we"). This policy explains how the game handles your data.

1. The game does not collect your personal data

  • No account sign-up or login is required.
  • Your game progress (chickens, eggs, coins, etc.) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. We do not run any servers and cannot access your game data.
  • The game does not use analytics tools and does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads, provided by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android) and other device identifiers
  • IP address, and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and system information (such as model, OS version, and language)
  • Ad interactions (such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped)
  • Diagnostics such as crash and performance data

Google uses this data under its own policies to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud and abuse. For details, see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Consent and tracking

  • In regions where consent is legally required (such as the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown the first time you open the game, and you can choose whether to give consent.
  • On iPhone, the game asks for permission for tracking. If you do not allow it, you can still play normally; you will just see less relevant (non-personalized) ads.
  • Watching ads is optional. You can play without watching any ads.

4. Your choices

  • Change your ad consent (in regions where consent is required): tap "?" at the top right of the game, then tap "Ad privacy settings" (廣告隱私設定) on the help screen.
  • Turn off tracking on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn off Chicken Farm.
  • Reset or delete your Android Advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Manage Google ad personalization: https://adssettings.google.com

5. Children

The game is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child's data has been collected, please contact us.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Game progress is stored on your device; it is deleted when you delete the game. You can also clear it by tapping "Restart" (重新開始) on the help screen in the game.
  • Data collected by AdMob is retained and processed by Google under its own policies, and you can manage it through the Google links above.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions, please email wantx94@gmail.com.
